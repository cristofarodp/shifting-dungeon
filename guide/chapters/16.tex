\section{16. The treasure}

Exactly one treasure room is selected from five possible appearances, preventing reliable identification through familiarity with previous adventures. It is safe from ordinary encounters and cannot collapse.

\subsection{Treasure room security}

All four directional approaches initially have special locked and trapped states overriding ordinary conditions. Unlike ordinary directional states, this security remains associated with its approaches even when surrounding chambers shift. Players must resolve appropriate security before entering through that direction. Once cleared, that approach remains cleared.

\subsection{Acquiring the treasure}

The treasure should be immediately recognizable as valuable but not necessarily authentic through appearance alone. Determine monetary value, magical properties or campaign significance. It need not be a powerful combat item; its principal role is motivation and the condition for successful escape. Entering the treasure room triggers doomsday after the current door closes and collapse checks are resolved.
