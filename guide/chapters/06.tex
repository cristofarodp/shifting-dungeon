\section{6. The shifting mechanic}

Whenever a door is successfully opened, the dungeon attempts to shift one row or column by one position in a randomly selected direction. A shift moves entire chambers, including their contents and occupants.

\subsection{Restrictions}

A shift cannot move the entrance, either chamber connected by the open door, or a chamber protected by an applicable magical anchor. After doomsday begins, a shift must also preserve connectivity between all remaining chambers. If no legal shift exists, the door opens normally without a shift.

\subsection{Narrating shifts}

Characters hear the movement of stone. Give information about direction and distance of the shifting row or column relative to their current position.

\begin{quote}\itshape A deep grinding sound echoes from somewhere two chambers to the north. You hear enormous blocks of stone sliding eastward before everything falls silent again.\end{quote}

The sound provides useful navigational information without revealing the complete layout. Characters inside moving chambers remain in them and are not damaged merely because the room moves.

\subsection{Manual and controlled shifts}

Certain magical objects allow players to influence or prevent shifts. These effects must respect ordinary structural restrictions. A controlled shift cannot move the entrance or disconnect the remaining dungeon. An illegal controlled shift does not consume the magical object. Distinguish player-controlled shifts from manual corrections to dungeon state.
