\section{8. Magical boundaries}

\subsection{Creatures}

Enemies cannot leave their own chamber. They may pursue characters to a doorway but cannot cross into another room. Players can escape without defeating every enemy. Apply this consistently, including to flying, incorporeal and teleporting enemies.

\subsection{Attacks and spells}

Attacks, projectiles and spell effects cannot pass through a doorway, even when open. Characters cannot stand safely in an adjacent room and shoot enemies through the opening, nor can enemies attack beyond their chamber. Thrown objects can pass through but land harmlessly in the first space on the opposite side, including objects that would ordinarily produce a harmful effect upon impact. This prevents neutralizing encounters without entering their rooms.

\subsection{Teleportation and unusual abilities}

The established rules do not exhaustively define every form of magical movement. A recommended ruling is that teleportation within a chamber functions normally, while magic cannot bypass boundaries or transport characters directly out unless explicitly permitted by a dungeon-specific effect. Apply similar reasoning to ethereal travel, extradimensional movement, tunnelling and structural destruction. Do not invalidate ordinary abilities unnecessarily; restrictions should protect central mechanics rather than arbitrarily weaken classes.
