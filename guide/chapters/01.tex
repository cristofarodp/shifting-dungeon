\section{1. Adventure overview}

Shifting Dungeon is a survival-oriented dungeon adventure in which the environment itself is the principal antagonist. Its chambers constantly rearrange themselves, making conventional navigation unreliable, while puzzles, encounters, traps and magical objects force players to make difficult decisions.

The adventurers have three primary objectives: explore the dungeon and understand its rules; locate and acquire the treasure; return to the entrance before the dungeon collapses around them.

Unlike a conventional dungeon crawl, clearing every room is neither necessary nor desirable. Combat is not always optimal, retreat is encouraged, and information can be more valuable than treasure. The adventure is built around uncertainty, discovery, resource management, cooperation and escalating pressure.

The companion application handles generation, spatial relationships, door states, discovery, player positions, collected rewards and structural changes. The Dungeon Master remains responsible for narration, combat, difficulty, adjudication and the overall experience.
