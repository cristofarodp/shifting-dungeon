\section{13. Treasure chests and rewards}

Ordinary chests appear in puzzle, easy fight and easy trap rooms. Each contains a key, a magical challenge reward and one adventurer's note, provided undiscovered notes remain. Puzzle chests require solving the puzzle. In tricky rooms, the DM may allow characters to obtain the chest without completely defeating the encounter or disabling the trap. Encourage distraction, stealth, rapid movement and cooperation.

\subsection{Reward distribution}

Challenge rewards are unique: once awarded, an item cannot appear in another chest in the same adventure. If all are collected, subsequent chests award a boss reward instead. Keys and boss rewards may appear multiple times. The application records collected objects; the DM tracks ownership, charges, consumption and actual use.

\subsection{Adjudicating magical objects}

Consult the current item descriptions for durations, charges and restrictions. Items should be useful without automatically solving the adventure. A Reality Anchor controls movement but does not inherently protect against collapse. The Thread of Ariadne reveals a route at activation; later shifts may invalidate it. Encourage creative combinations as part of the intended strategic depth.
