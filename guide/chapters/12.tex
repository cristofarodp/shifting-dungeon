\section{12. Room resets}

A room resets when it becomes empty of player characters, subject to these exceptions.

\begin{longtable}{p{0.34\linewidth}p{0.6\linewidth}}

\rowcolor{tablehead} \textbf{Room element} & \textbf{Resets?} \\

\endfirsthead

\rowcolor{tablehead} \textbf{Room element} & \textbf{Resets?} \\

\endhead

Ordinary enemies & Yes \\

Ordinary room traps & Yes \\

Death traps & Yes \\

Unfinished puzzle progress & Yes \\

Solved puzzles & No \\

Defeated bosses & No \\

Collected chest rewards & No \\

Resolved door traps & No \\

Permanently collapsed rooms & No \\

\end{longtable}

Enemies return to their original condition. A party cannot reliably defeat ordinary encounters by repeatedly inflicting small amounts of damage and retreating. A character remaining inside prevents the ordinary reset, creating opportunities for coordinated tactics at the cost of exposure. A defeated boss stays dead and its reward cannot be repeatedly collected. An emptied chest stays empty even if the encounter resets.

\subsection{Narrating resets}

Let players discover this naturally. When they revisit a cleared chamber, describe restored enemies or mechanisms.

\begin{quote}\itshape The shattered bones are no longer scattered across the floor. They stand upright once more, arranged exactly as they were when you first entered.\end{quote}

The dungeon should feel as though it is reconstructing its own challenges.
