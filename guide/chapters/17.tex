\section{17. Illusion rooms}

Some dangerous chambers may be illusion rooms. They resemble the treasure room but conceal a trap or special behavior. Their inclusion is not guaranteed. Consult the selected room description and present the deception convincingly, allowing appropriate investigation or magic to reveal it.

For example, Fool's Fortune offers illusory treasure and may simulate doomsday. The deception ends when the false treasure reaches the entrance or the party enters the treasure room. Apparent collapses do not permanently alter the dungeon.
