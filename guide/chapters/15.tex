\section{15. Adventurers' notes}

Ten unique notes written by previous adventurers communicate mechanics through in-world observations. Each eligible chest awards one randomly selected undiscovered note. After all ten are found, further chests contain no additional notes.

The Moving Walls — Door openings cause distant shifts

The World Without Edges — The dungeon wraps around itself

A Lock Has Two Faces — Door states can differ by direction

Six Seconds — Doors close rapidly; only one opens at a time

The Returning Dead — Ordinary enemies reset, defeated bosses do not

Beyond the Threshold — Enemies and attacks cannot cross doors

The Last Chambers — Excessive exploration triggers doomsday

Fool's Fortune — A false treasure exists

The Way Home — Empty rooms collapse during doomsday

One Life for Many — Resurrection Rune mechanics

\subsection{Presenting notes}

Read a note aloud or allow players to read its complete text. Do not immediately explain implications; let them interpret information and compare it with experience. Notes can significantly change strategy: a group warned about the final unexplored chambers may limit exploration, while a group learning about false treasure may investigate suspicious riches. Random distribution means different groups acquire information at different stages. Do not guarantee every important mechanic will be explained before it becomes relevant; environmental observations should also provide clues.
