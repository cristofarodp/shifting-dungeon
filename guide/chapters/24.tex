\section{Appendix B — Preparation checklist}

\ensuremath{\square} Generate and review the dungeon.

\ensuremath{\square} Prepare suitable enemy and boss statistics.

\ensuremath{\square} Assign trap damage and saving throw DCs.

\ensuremath{\square} Review selected puzzle solutions.

\ensuremath{\square} Determine treasure's value and significance.

\ensuremath{\square} Review magical item effects and usage restrictions.

\ensuremath{\square} Prepare character names and starting resources.

\ensuremath{\square} Establish rulings for resting and extradimensional magic.

\ensuremath{\square} Decide how individual escape and abandoned companions are handled.

\ensuremath{\square} Keep player-facing descriptions separate from DM information.
